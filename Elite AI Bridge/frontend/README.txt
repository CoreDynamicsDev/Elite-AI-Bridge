Elite AI Bridge 2.0 interface (Qt Quick / QML).

main.py starts the boot splash, the backend (backend/backend_host.py) and the QML
interface in qml/. Pages are in qml/pages, the shared deck parts in qml/skin and the
header, module column and setup wizard in qml/shell.

Launch with RUN_CURRENT_SOURCE.bat in the source root, or the installed shortcut.
