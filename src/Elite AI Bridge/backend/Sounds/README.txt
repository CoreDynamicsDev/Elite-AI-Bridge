Elite AI Bridge v0.12.64 Retro Audio Pack

These bundled WAV files are original procedural audio generated specifically for the Bridge. No third-party audio samples are embedded in these files.

Design target: late-1970s science-fiction control room, cassette/tape transport, relay hardware, muted analog oscillators, and restrained warning tones. The pack intentionally avoids making every event a bright chirp.

Files:
  startup.wav             tape/relay boot sequence
  interdiction.wav        low electromechanical emergency klaxon
  danger.wav              low double warning pulse
  docking_granted.wav     relay + warm upward confirm
  docking_denied.wav      relay + low descending reject
  route_ready.wav         subdued contactor/navigation confirm
  crew_payment.wav        mechanical register + muted bell
  automation_confirm.wav  short relay/contact click
  attention.wav           calm two-tone pre-voice earcon

Use Preferences > Sound Effects > BROWSE / IMPORT to assign replacements. The Bridge copies custom 16-bit PCM WAVs into %LOCALAPPDATA%\EliteAIBridge\Sounds\Custom and leaves these factory files untouched.
