CC0 / Public-Domain Audio Research Notes
Elite AI Bridge v0.12.62

The v0.12.62 bundled WAV files are NOT copied from these sources. They are original procedural sounds generated specifically for Elite AI Bridge, with no third-party audio samples embedded.

These CC0 libraries were researched as possible future replacement/source material:

1. OpenGameArt - Retro Computer Noise (aquinn)
   https://opengameart.org/content/retro-computer-noise
   License listed by OpenGameArt: CC0
   Description specifically references retro sci-fi computers with lights and spinning reels.

2. OpenGameArt - UI Sounds (HaelDB)
   https://opengameart.org/content/ui-sounds-0
   License listed by OpenGameArt: CC0
   Includes error, accept, notification and attention-style UI sounds.

3. OpenGameArt - Interface Sounds (Kenney)
   https://opengameart.org/content/interface-sounds
   License listed by OpenGameArt: CC0
   100 normalized interface/button/switch sounds.

4. OpenGameArt - 60 CC0 Sci-Fi SFX (rubberduck)
   https://opengameart.org/node/97882
   License listed by OpenGameArt: CC0
   Sci-fi / terminal / metal / ambient sounds.

Reason they are not bundled in this build: the development environment could verify the source/license pages but could not reliably retrieve the binary ZIP/audio payloads. No external sound was included without actually obtaining and auditing the file.
